03

Accent ramp from one hex

The consultant enters one color. Everything else is computed: tints for selection and wells, a contrast-safe solid for buttons, an on-accent text color, accent text for links, and a focus ring. Shown for the three sample firms.

1Read the hex in OKLCHLightness, chroma and hue are separated so we can move lightness without the hue drifting.
2Build tints at fixed lightness50–300 sit at fixed L with chroma scaled down, so every brand gets equally quiet tints.
3Fit the solid to 4.5:1If white text on the exact brand fails, lightness steps down until it passes. The consultant sees "Adjusted for contrast."
4Re-fit for dark modeSolids move into the band that clears 3:1 against the dark base and 4.5:1 with white text; accent text lifts to 4.8:1.

Status colors

Success, warning, danger and info never borrow the brand ramp. If a brand hue lands within 30° of a status hue, the status hue moves away (Evergreen’s success leans yellow-green; Bloom’s danger leans crimson). Every status also carries an icon and a word, so color is never the only signal.
